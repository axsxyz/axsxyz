### axs

Software engineer. 
I like taking something that works on my machine and making it run on its own: deployed, observed, and honest about its limits.

**How I work**

- **Agent-first.** Coding agents do much of the typing, under explicit rules, invariants and tests. My part is the design, the review, and knowing how to judge what they produce.
- **Measure before I claim.** A number I publish points to a run, a test or a commit.
- **Small steps, each proven.** I don't build on a step until I have evidence it works.
- **Write down what broke.** Incidents, root causes and open items stay visible. A project can be live with open items, as long as they are tracked.
